# Poster benchmark boards — the built-in visual benchmark

Four boards, roughly forty contemporary human-made posters (editorial,
exhibition, music, cultural), at `assets/benchmarks/poster-board-1.png`
through `poster-board-4.png`. These are the references that produced the
step-change in the 夜班之后 case (`optional/case-night-shift-poster.md`).

They exist because a visual standard cannot be learned from prose. Text
rules can say "use halftone" or "build a type system"; only looking at
strong work teaches what those words are supposed to look like. When the
benchmark obligation runs and the user supplied no references, **open and
view all four images** — reading this file without viewing the boards does
not discharge the obligation.

## How to use

1. View all four boards.
2. While viewing, fill the calibration table from
   `workflow/density-and-care.md` with what you actually see: type-level
   counts and scale ratios, operations inside images, elements added outside
   images, line/icon/shape and material systems, dense and quiet zones,
   visual-weight distribution.
3. Extract devices, never surfaces. The goal is to steal the *mechanism* and
   re-derive it from the current subject.

## What the boards teach — with visible examples

- **Display type constructed as imagery.** 就是饼 JIANBING FESTIVAL (board 3)
  — the type construction *is* the poster; Grass LAKE (board 4) — outline
  display type woven over a landscape photo; 不曾存在 Existed Nowhere
  (board 4) — giant bilingual stack with mirror symmetry. Type is not a
  label on the design; it is the design.
- **Real type systems with annotation energy.** 坐一艘慢船去春天 (board 4) —
  bilingual editorial grid with timetable-grade small type that is factual
  and crafted; AVANT CHAIR (board 2) — date blocks, map, and schedule as
  composition; ★POP UP★ BURGERS & MATCHA (board 2) — price-list voice,
  every level a different job.
- **Print materiality.** Bloom (board 1) — image built from coarse halftone;
  CAUGHT IN TIME (board 3) — newsprint grain and overprint; THIS WAY
  (board 1) — photocopy red on blueprint. Material is executed inside the
  image, not overlaid on it.
- **Image operations and collage.** 台灣山岳 (board 2) — painterly collage
  with a vertical caption column; 放风 (board 3) — display type overlapping
  and cropping the photograph; SONNENSTUB (board 2) — one decisive crop,
  sky as field.
- **Scale courage and one electric color event.** CLASSY FRIDAY (board 2) —
  flat red field, giant black sans, one blue heart; dublon (board 2) — one
  blue arch over a full-bleed photo. Bold does not mean busy.
- **Irregular / hand-drawn voice.** Player One Party OPEN (board 1),
  春光掠影 FASHION SHOW (board 4), GOAT Spatial Fes (board 4) — looseness
  as an authored register, not a lack of craft.

## What to refuse

- Copying any specific motif, palette, font, mascot, layout, or slogan from
  these boards. The moment a device is traceable to one board image instead
  of to your subject, it is plagiarism, not calibration.
- Importing the boards' noise: they are screenshot collages with UI chrome,
  watermarks, and mixed quality. Judge the posters, not the screenshots.
- Treating the loudest boards as the standard. Several teach restraint
  through one decisive move; pick what serves your subject.

## Boundary

Benchmark images never enter the deliverable — they calibrate the eye.
User-supplied references take precedence and are decomposed with
`optional/reference-synthesis.md`; these boards are the floor when none are
given, alongside `references/style-evidence.md` and
`optional/case-night-shift-poster.md`.
