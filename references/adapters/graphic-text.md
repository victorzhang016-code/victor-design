# Graphic-text and social editorial adapter

Use for a cover plus body pages forming a continuous article, carousel, or
social reading flow. If an image is attached, resolve its role before layout.
The set is not one poster repeated and not a stack of generic cards.

## Sequence contract

Lock the source order, page count or capacity, platform ratio, reading context,
and editable delivery. Define four page jobs:

- **cover:** poster-level identity and promise;
- **body:** one earned idea with its evidence and enough visual treatment;
- **transition:** a real change of chapter, evidence mode, time, or voice;
- **closing:** resolves the reading path with a fact, decision, or earned
  residue.

Text flows continuously in source order. Break pages at capacity or an earned
beat, never to manufacture rhythm.

## Aesthetic completion

Benchmarking is required, not optional: select adjacent human-made editorial,
magazine, campaign, or social references and run
`optional/reference-synthesis.md`; when the user supplies none, calibrate
against the built-in base in `references/style-evidence.md`. Inherit hierarchy,
pacing, image treatment, and relational density; do not import a platform
template.

The cover may use display typography, decisive image treatment, and dense
local composition. Body pages prioritize reading while remaining authored:
factual quotations, captions, diagrams, image details, recurring lines, icons,
or graphic marks can carry continuity. Do not turn every page into a competing
poster and do not let body pages collapse into rounded-card templates.

When material is thin, add only:

- `factual` source copy, quotes, captions, or evidence;
- `interpretive draft` headings or transitions awaiting approval;
- `decorative/semantic` devices that structure the article and claim no fact.

## Density by page job

- Cover: dominant event plus multi-distance reading.
- Body: conclusion/idea, supporting content, and at least two crafted relations.
- Transition: one clear semantic change; high scale is allowed, emptiness is
  earned only when it resets rhythm.
- Closing: the final evidence or statement plus a designed residue.

Every high-fidelity page meets the design-act density target from
`workflow/density-and-care.md`; repeated furniture counts once unless it
changes a page's reading.

## Density sync with the poster standard

- The cover executes the poster density targets from
  `workflow/density-and-care.md` (type levels, anchor regions, image
  operations, design acts).
- The thin-brief playbook and the benchmark obligation in
  `workflow/density-and-care.md` apply to every body page, not only to
  posters; a thin brief is a production task, never permission for a bare
  page.

## Pilot and review

Make a role map, then build the cover, densest body page, and most unusual page
as full-craft pilots. Compare them against references at equal scale. Inspect
each page, a thumbnail contact sheet, and the full reading sequence before
batch production and delivery.
